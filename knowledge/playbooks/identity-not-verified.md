# Playbook: Identity Not Verified

## Trigger
Identity verification returns verified = false with confidence of 0.8 or higher, meaning the provider is confident the identity does not match.

## Action
The application is rejected. The applicant is invited to visit a branch with their original ID if they believe this is an error. Do not suggest that fraud is suspected.
