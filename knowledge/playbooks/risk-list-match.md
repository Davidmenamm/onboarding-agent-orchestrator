# Playbook: Risk List Match

## High risk (sanctions or strong watchlist match)
Escalate to Compliance through the analyst queue. Attach the match details and similarity scores. Most name-similarity matches are false positives; Compliance confirms or clears them using date of birth, nationality and document number. The account must not be opened until Compliance clears the match.

## Medium risk (partial or PEP-related match)
Proceed with enhanced due diligence. No analyst review is required unless the product policy says so.

## Tipping-off prohibition
Never disclose to the applicant that a sanctions or watchlist match occurred. Use "your application is under review" wording.
