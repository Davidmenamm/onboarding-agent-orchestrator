# Data Protection Policy for Onboarding

## Principles
Data minimization, purpose limitation and least privilege. Personal data is collected only to onboard the applicant.

## Storage
Names and national ID numbers are encrypted at rest. Lookups use a keyed hash. Analysts see masked values and must give a reason to reveal them; every reveal is audited.

## External processors
Personal data must not be sent to external AI services. Only pseudonymized data may leave the bank's infrastructure.

## Retention
Personal data of rejected or abandoned applications is deleted after 90 days.
