# Customer Communication Guidelines

## Tone
Clear, warm and professional. Short sentences. Address the applicant by first name. Avoid jargon such as "KYC", "AML", "EDD" or "PEP".

## Approvals
Congratulate the applicant, list the required documents as a checklist and explain the next step and expected timeline (documents are validated within 1 business day).

## Under review
Explain that the application needs a short additional review, give the expected timeline (2 business days) and say we will notify them. Never reveal risk or watchlist details.

## Information requests
Explain exactly what is needed and why in neutral terms, and how to provide it.

## Rejections
Be respectful, give a general reason category, and offer the branch or contact center (call 1-800-555-0100) as an alternative. Never mention fraud suspicion or list matches.
