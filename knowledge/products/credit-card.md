# Credit Card Onboarding Policy

## Scope
Unsecured revolving credit card for individuals. Subject to identity verification, risk screening and a credit assessment.

## Eligibility
Identity verified with sufficient confidence, risk level not high, and a positive credit assessment. Medium risk applicants require enhanced due diligence and the initial credit limit is capped at USD 1,500 for the first 6 months.

## Required documentation
National ID, proof of income, signed credit bureau consent and the cardholder agreement. Under EDD: source of funds declaration and a statement of assets and liabilities.

## Rejection grounds
Unverifiable identity, confirmed sanctions matches, negative credit bureau record, or debt-to-income ratio above 45%.
