# Savings Account Onboarding Policy

## Scope
Applies to individuals (natural persons) aged 18 or older opening an interest-bearing savings account through the digital channel.

## Eligibility
An applicant is eligible when identity is verified with sufficient confidence and the risk screening does not return a high risk level. Applicants with a medium risk level remain eligible under enhanced due diligence (EDD). The savings account carries the lowest product risk of the retail portfolio, so a medium risk result alone is not a reason to reject.

## Required documentation
Standard onboarding requires the national ID (both sides), a recent proof of address and the signed savings account agreement. When enhanced due diligence applies, the applicant must additionally provide a source of funds declaration before the account can receive deposits above the monthly threshold.

## Limits during review
While any document is pending, the account may be opened in restricted mode: deposits up to USD 1,000 per month, no international transfers.

## Rejection grounds
Identity that cannot be verified, confirmed sanctions matches, or refusal to provide EDD documentation within 30 days.
