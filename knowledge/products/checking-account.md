# Checking Account Onboarding Policy

## Scope
Applies to individuals opening a transactional checking account with debit card and checkbook through the digital channel.

## Eligibility
Identity must be verified with sufficient confidence. Because checking accounts allow third-party payments and checkbooks, medium risk applicants require enhanced due diligence including a bank reference letter. High risk applicants must be reviewed by Compliance before any decision.

## Required documentation
National ID, recent proof of address, proof of income and the signed checking account agreement. Under EDD the applicant also provides a source of funds declaration and a bank reference letter.

## Checkbook issuance
Checkbooks are issued only after all documents, including EDD documents, are received and validated.

## Rejection grounds
Unverifiable identity, confirmed sanctions matches, returned-check history reported by the credit bureau, or falsified documents.
