# Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Guide

## When EDD applies
EDD applies when risk screening returns a medium risk level, when the applicant is a politically exposed person or a close relative of one, or when an analyst requests it after a review.

## What EDD requires
The applicant must provide a source of funds declaration and any product-specific EDD documents listed in the product policy. The account may operate in restricted mode until EDD documents are validated.

## Ongoing monitoring
EDD customers are subject to quarterly transaction monitoring for the first year.

## Communication
The applicant must not be told that they appear on a PEP or watch list. Use neutral wording such as "additional documentation is required by our onboarding policy".
